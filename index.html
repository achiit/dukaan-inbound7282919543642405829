Hello, I am Rakesh Gupta.

My father, Late Shri Mohan Lal Gupta, started this shop in 1994 with one kadhai and a small counter near the Lal Kuan crossing. I still remember carrying the first tray of ladoos to the neighbours on Diwali morning.

My uncle Sunil taught me how to judge sugar syrup by touch, not by thermometer. He still stands at the jalebi kadhai at 5 AM, and I would not trust anyone else with it.

We do not run a chain and we do not use shortcuts. If a customer's grandmother says the ladoo tastes like before, that is our best day.

  • Pure desi ghee, never vanaspati
  • Made fresh daily and never sold the next day
  • Sugar-light options for diabetic customers
  • Home delivery within Lal Kuan and nearby colonies
  • Same family, same recipes, same counter
